👩‍🎓 About Me

Hi, I am Wenxue Li, a Senior Researcher (Alibaba Star) at Alibaba Group. My recent work focuses on improving network performance and reliability for large-scale AI training and inference in production clusters.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) in 2026, where I was advised by Prof. Kai Chen. Prior to that, I received my B.Sc. in Electronic Science and Technology from Zhejiang University in 2021.

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in my work.
